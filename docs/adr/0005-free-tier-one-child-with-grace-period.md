# Free tier covers one child and two parents; extra children get a 14-day grace period then become read-only for parents

Premium is sold to parents after they are invested, and the child's side is never paywalled. Free households get one child, two parents, unlimited chores, the pet, built-in rewards and 7 days of history. During onboarding a parent may add all their children; children beyond the quota keep working fully for the child (pet, streak, completions) and after 14 days only the parent's ability to edit that child's chores locks until premium. Premium also gates a third parent, custom rewards, the money ledger and payouts, full history and photo proof. Pricing: $39.99/yr, $6.99/mo, $79.99 lifetime, no trial, paywall shown only at a gate. Entitlement is enforced server-side and mirrored to devices for offline.

This is the decision most likely to produce "can't use it without paying" reviews; it was chosen with that risk explicit and is watched via the paywall-shown analytics event.
